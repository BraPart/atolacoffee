A quiet corner of Al Mansourah, made for slow cups and long evenings

Atole Cafe began with a simple idea: serve drinks you would be proud to share with family and friends, in a room where everyone feels welcome. Every glass of karak is brewed strong and creamy, every latte is poured with care, and every guest is greeted like a regular.

More than 125 guests have rated us 4.9 out of 5. They come for the Spanish latte and stay for the atmosphere, the service and the late-night calm, since we are open until 2:00 AM every day.
